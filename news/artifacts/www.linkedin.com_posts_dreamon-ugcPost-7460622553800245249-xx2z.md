Meet us at Biblioteksgatan 16 – we're open in Stockholm. 🇸🇪

Situated in the dynamic Bibliotekstan district, our first store in Sweden opens its doors today. 

Pulling inspiration from the iconic geometry of Sergels Torg, a sweeping floor of native Swedish stone is balanced by warm, textured walls and historic arches, merging with On’s signature clean, technical finish of polished steel. Spanning more than 250 sqm, our full product portfolio is there for you – from footwear to apparel, and accessories for high-performance running, outdoor activities, training, all-day and tennis. 

Ready to welcome you Monday through Sunday, and at weekly run club, connecting at the store every Wednesday evening at 6pm. 👋
