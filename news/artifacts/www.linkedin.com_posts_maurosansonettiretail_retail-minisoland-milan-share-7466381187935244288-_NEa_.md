From vision to reality: The first MINISO LAND in Europe is officially coming to Milan! 🇮🇹🇪🇺

I am thrilled to announce that we have officially closed negotiations and signed the contract for the iconic ex-Hamley’s location in Corso Vittorio Emanuele II, Milan.

In December 2026, this spectacular space will welcome the very first MINISO LAND in Europe 🇪🇺✨ — Miniso’s most prestigious and immersive retail format.

Leading this groundbreaking project is an absolute honor, and I couldn't be prouder of our incredible team for making this vision a reality.

A heartfelt thank you to the owner Mr. Guglielmo Tabacchi, to Mrs. Francesca Basso, and to Mr. Tony for their trust, collaboration, and support throughout this journey.

Milan, get ready. Something extraordinary is coming! 🇨🇳🤝🇮🇹
