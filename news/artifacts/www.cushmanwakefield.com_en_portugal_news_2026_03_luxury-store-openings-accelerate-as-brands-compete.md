NYUNYU World has opened in Seoul's Myeongdong district as the brand's largest store to date, anchored by the vintage hotel aesthetic that has defined NYUNNYU Girls' visual identity since the women's clothing line launched.

The brand began as Korea's first accessories SPA brand, growing from the domestic wholesale market before expanding aggressively into multi-category retail. The Myeongdong flagship follows locations in Seongsu, Gangnam, and Dongdaemun, with the brand steadily broadening its product scope from accessories into women's clothing, fashion goods, and now in-store services. The new B1 NeuNeu Lab Eye Zone offers contact lenses and prescription glasses ready in under 30 minutes, a direct move into the fast-service optical category.

The timing connects to a wider shift in Korean offline retail. South Korea's total sales across major retailers rose 7.9% year-on-year in February 2026, with department stores up 25.6%, per the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources. Myeongdong remains the country's most visited shopping corridor by inbound tourists, with Korea projected to receive 20 to 21 million foreign visitors in 2026 according to Yanolja Research.
