See Fashion's Most Exciting Store Openings in 2026

Shopping just got a whole lot chicer in 2026. L'OFFICIEL rounds up all the must-know store openings around the globe in 2026. 

Published 03.03.2026 by L'OFFICIEL USA
Last updated on 04.18.2026
Ryan Gosling at Tag Heuer's 99 Prince St. Boutique. Courtesy of Tag Heuer.

Retail is having a renaissance. In 2026, brands aren’t just opening doors; they’re building legacies. From downtown New York flagships to sun-soaked West Coast concept spaces and buzzy international outposts abroad, this year’s chicest store openings prove that brick-and-mortar is anything but ordinary. These destinations blend fashion, art, and immersive design, transforming shopping into a full-sensory experience.

 Ahead, we round up the most stylish new spaces redefining retail across the globe. Consider this your passport to 2026’s must-visit stores.

New York City 
Issey Miyake 
Renderings of Issey Miyake's new store at 45 Madison Ave. Courtesy of Issey Miyake.

Issey Miyake is expanding its New York footprint with the opening of a new flagship at 45 Madison Avenue on May 8, 2026. Set inside the historic New York Life Building, the space occupies a prime corner overlooking Madison Square Park, bringing the brand’s signature fusion of innovation and craftsmanship into one of the city’s most architecturally rich neighborhoods.

Spanning 13,000 square feet across two levels, the store—designed by New York-based firm Solid Objectives Idenburg Liu—leans into a dialogue between past and present. Original Beaux-Arts elements of the building have been carefully preserved and juxtaposed with sleek aluminum and stainless-steel finishes, striking a balance between heritage and modernity. At the center, a striking transparent glass staircase anchors the space, showcasing the level of engineering precision that mirrors Miyake’s design ethos. Flooded with natural light from expansive windows on three sides, the flagship feels deeply connected to its surroundings, offering a bright, immersive retail experience that reflects both the brand’s forward-thinking vision and its respect for craftsmanship.

Tag Heuer 
Tag Heuer Boutique at 99 Prince St. Courtesy of Tag Heuer.

Swiss watchmaker Tag Heuer is doubling down on downtown cool with the opening of its newest flagship in SoHo, planting roots at 99 Prince Street. The sleek, 57.2-square-meter space introduces the brand’s latest retail concept—where heritage meets modern performance—with a curated lineup of signature collections like the Carrera, Monaco, and Formula 1, alongside limited-edition releases.

To celebrate the launch, brand ambassador Ryan Gosling visited the boutique, stepping inside for an up-close look at the collection and even trying his hand at watchmaking. The space itself leans into immersion, blending contemporary design with subtle nods to T Heuer’s Swiss legacy, while offering clients a deeper dive into the craftsmanship and innovation behind each timepiece.

Fleur du Mal 
Fleur du Mal store at 372 Broome St. Photography by Andy Martinez, courtesy of Fleur du Mal.

Fleur du Mal marked a new chapter in its retail expansion with the opening of its downtown flagship at 372 Broome Street on March 11. Set in Nolita, where the brand first found its footing. Guests sipped on cocktails as House of Yes dancers moved through the crowd, offering Alice Mushrooms sexual wellness chocolates and cacao shots designed to “open the heart.” 

The space itself reflects founder Jennifer Zuccarini’s full vision: a refined yet provocative ode to femininity, sensuality, and intentional dressing. The night drew a stylish crowd including Zuccarini, Angelica Hicks, Anna Delvey, Diana Veras, and Gabriella Richardson, cementing the boutique as both a retail destination and a cultural touchpoint in downtown New York.

Susan Alexandra 
Susan Alexandra Store in the Upper East Side. Photography BFA, courtesy of Susan Alexandra.

Susan Alexandra is moving up, the Upper East Side, to be exact. The larger-than-life personality of a boutique is now open at 1088 Madison Ave. Designer Susan Korn welcomed guests into her newly unveiled store. Designed in collaboration with Studio Galeon, the boutique reflects Korn’s distinct, joy-forward vision down to the finest detail. Handmade mirrors by Barbini punctuate the space, while intricately beaded sconces and chandeliers cast a soft, romantic glow overhead. True to the brand’s identity, its signature yellow—brought to life with paint courtesy of Behr—washes the interiors in warmth, creating an immersive environment that feels both playful and polished.

Dôen 
Dôen on Madison Ave. Courtesy of Dôen.

Dôen is continuing its retail expansion with the opening of its Madison Avenue boutique, marking the Los Angeles–based brand’s eighth location and second in New York City. Situated at 1013 Madison Avenue between 78th and 79th Streets, the 1,036-square-foot space arrives during Dôen's 10th anniversary year, adding an elevated Upper East Side address to its growing East Coast presence. Designed once again by longtime collaborators Amy Kehoe and Todd Nickey of Nickey Kehoe, the boutique blends decorative moldings inspired by Versailles with the brand’s unmistakable romantic, lived-in charm. Inside, shoppers will find Dôen’s signature ready-to-wear, accessories, intimates, sleepwear, and children’s pieces—all housed in a jewel-box setting. 

Milan 
Ann Demeulemeester
Ann Demeulemeester Store in Milan. Courtesy of Ann Demeulemeester.

In the heart of Quadrilatero della Moda, Ann Demeulemeester has officially opened its newest, chicest boutique at Via Monte Napoleone 22. The boutique is brought to life, capturing the ethos Stefano Gallici has striven to create for the brand. The store brings to life the brand's heritage while maintaining a modern ease in the boutique. 

This is Ann Demeulemeester's second mono-brand store in the world, following its flagship located in Antwerp. 

Paris 
Roger Vivier 
Roger Vivier boutique at 20 rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré. Courtesy of Roger Vivier.

Roger Vivier has unveiled a new Paris flagship at 20 rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, reinforcing its roots in the city where founder Roger Vivier first established the house. Spanning two floors, the boutique blends heritage and design with a richly layered interior—think French stone, plush textiles, and curated artworks—while introducing Atelier Vivier, a bespoke service offering personalized shoes, handbags, and bridal pieces. Positioned alongside Maison Vivier, the new space cements the brand’s dual focus on culture and client experience.

Los Angeles 
Revolve 
Revolve at The Grove. Courtesy of Revolve.

The Grove welcomed Revolve for the grand opening of their flagship store—marking a new chapter for the digital fashion powerhouse. The space signals the next evolution of Revolve's commitment to elevated retail, bringing its online universe into a tangible, design-forward environment. Alongside a curated mix of covetable pieces from both Revolve and Fwrd, the flagship spotlights established names and emerging designers across apparel, footwear, accessories, beauty, and home. Located at 189 The Grove Drive, the new store cements Revolve's physical footprint in one of Los Angeles’ most iconic shopping destinations.
