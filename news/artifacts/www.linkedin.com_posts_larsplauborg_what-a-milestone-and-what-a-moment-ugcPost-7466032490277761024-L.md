What a milestone — and what a moment!🥂

We are incredibly proud and excited to share a very special achievement in BOLIA’s journey:

Today, we have opened store number 100! 🎉

Present in 13 countries, this is a truly significant milestone for us — a moment that reflects years of passion, craftsmanship, collaboration, and dedication across all our teams. 

And yet, it is still only a step on the journey, as we move forward with great ambition towards the next 100 stores, driven by our vision of making BOLIA a truly global design brand for even more design lovers around the world.

This new store, beautifully located at Limmatquai Zürich in a highly attractive corner position, represents not only our continued growth, but also a clear proof point of our success in the city. 

Exactly 10 years after opening our first store in Zürich, we are now expanding with a second location — a strong testament to the relevance of our design universe and the dedication of our teams.

Reaching 100 stores is something we can all be deeply proud of. It reflects years of ambition, collaboration, and commitment across markets, functions, and colleagues. 

Every opening carries its own story, but this one stands out as a symbol of how far we’ve come — and how much potential lies ahead.

A special thank you to our fantastic colleagues in Zürich, who will now bring this beautiful space to life every day, welcoming customers and sharing the BOLIA experience. 🇨🇭

We look forward to celebrating this milestone together — and to many more inspiring openings to come.

BOLIA - Tailoring Your Dreams 🤍
