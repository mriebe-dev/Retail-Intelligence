Sézane Opening in Georgetown
GENERAL   •   APRIL 23, 2026
SHARE

Two years after launching a Georgetown pop-up, Sézane is opening a permanent store this Friday at 1242 Wisconsin Ave NW. Celebrate all weekend with a Grafika coffee cart pop-up and Dolcezza gelato, plus gifts with purchase while supplies last. According to their website, Sézane is inspired by Paris – each collec­tion a conver­sa­tion with the city itself and an ode to liberty. Shop the brand’s latest collections and iconic essentials, as well as its vintage-inspired jewelry selection and French lifestyle finds.
