Falconeri - 764 Madison Avenue is open.

This wasn't just a real estate decision. It was a declaration.

Madison Avenue is our third Manhattan address, and the centerpiece of something bigger. In 2026, Falconeri is bringing its cashmere revolution to the US: Valley Fair, Short Hills, Madison Avenue, Michigan Avenue, Boston. Seven locations by year end.

2,000 square feet on one of the world's most iconic retail corridors, designed around a single belief: cashmere must be felt to be truly understood. Oak millwork, warm materials, a library corner where the city slows down for a moment.

Because Falconeri's Ultrafine Cashmere is seasonless - not a winter layer, not a summer indulgence, but a 365-day fabric that travels with you through every season. That's a different conversation about cashmere.

To every partner and advisor who helped bring 764 to life — thank you.

There's a lot more to come.
