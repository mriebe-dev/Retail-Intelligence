🤝 Transaction | New Balance opens store on Lijnbaan 171 in Rotterdam

With the arrival of New Balance, the retail offering in the heart of the city is further strengthened. The international sports and lifestyle brand will open a store of approximately 850 sqm. 

The property has recently been renovated and made more sustainable, including a redesigned façade, an optimized store layout, and the installation of solar panels. 

The building is expected to be delivered with a BREEAM Excellent certification and to comply with Paris Proof standards. CBRE Nederland advised New Balance on this transaction. a.s.r. real estate was represented by Main Real Estate and Cushman & Wakefield, with Hemwood acting as legal advisor. Project management and technical supervision were handled by FiMek estate.
