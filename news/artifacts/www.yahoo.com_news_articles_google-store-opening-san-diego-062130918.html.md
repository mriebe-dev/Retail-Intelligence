Google Store opening in San Diego: Here are the details
Sharisse Cohee
Fri, May 8, 2026 at 6:21 AM UTC
1 min read
Add Yahoo as a preferred source to see more of our stories on Google.

SAN DIEGO (FOX 5/KUSI) — The world’s largest online search engine is setting up a physical shop in America’s Finest City.

Fashion Valley Mall will soon be home to the first Google Store in San Diego County, marking the second physical store in Southern California. The other store is located in Santa Monica.

The San Diego location will mark Google’s 10th physical retail store.

The Fashion Valley store opens Friday, May 8, giving San Diegans a space to test Google products hands-on. Featured products in the store include Pixel phones, Nest cameras, Fitbit Trackers and more.

The store will be open every day from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m., except on Sunday, when it closes at 7 p.m.

Google opened its first store in New York in June 2021 and has since received positive feedback regarding the hands-on experience the store offers.

The 3,997-square-foot store at the Fashion Valley Mall will offer more than just hands-on product testing. Patrons can shop via the online Google Store and then pick up their orders at the physical store.

The Fashion Valley location will also have experts on hand to help visitors with their devices, which include troubleshooting issues, providing Pixel phone repairs and more.
