Bravissimo! We've opened at the iconic Hotel La Palma in Capri, an Oekter Collection property, marking a refined new chapter for the brand in one of the Mediterranean’s most storied destinations.

You'll find a bright, new O.B store on either side of the Hotel La Palma entrance, 
which sits at the epicentre of Capri's social scene, just steps from the Piazzetta. 

This summer, we think everyone should follow the words of Prince Francesco Caravita di Sirignano:
"There are two ways to see the world: one is by travelling; the other is by sitting on the terrace at La Palma and waiting for the world to pass in front of you."

We look forward to welcoming you to O.B in Capri this summer.
