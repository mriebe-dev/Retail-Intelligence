Amiri

Amiri has established its flagship location in the United Kingdom, its second spot in Europe, further cementing the luxury brand as an international powerhouse.

Located at 123 New Bond Street, the shop’s decor blends old Hollywood luxe with UK tradition across tailored fashion and cinematic interior design. Spanning two floors are collections for men and women, including accessories and footwear.

Along with vintage furnishings is a desert-tone palette and strong Art Deco influence that complements the vision of founder and fashion extraordinaire Mike Amiri. With a West Coast vibrancy of rich shades of brown and cream along the shop, situated on the walls are plush upholsteries and large-scale tapestry artworks that provide a glamorous backdrop.
